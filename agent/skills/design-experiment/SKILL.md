---
name: design-experiment
description: Design a small real-game intervention to distinguish competing explanations of an observed effect.
---

Record alternative conditional hypotheses and their different predicted outcomes. Choose an available action whose observable result distinguishes them. Refer to real experience IDs, and keep unobserved mechanisms uncertain. Avoid changing multiple things when a single probe suffices. A repeated unchanged action is not new evidence unless testing a bounded delay. Report actual effects separately from goal progress. Use submit_decision with kind act; use the next real observation to update the hypotheses.
